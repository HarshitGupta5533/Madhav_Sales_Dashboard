# 📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, payment modes, and regional performance.

The dashboard provides a clear business overview through interactive filters and visualizations, helping users identify sales trends and profitable product segments.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to build an interactive sales dashboard that helps businesses:

- Track overall sales and profit performance
- Analyze monthly profit and loss trends
- Identify top-performing states
- Understand product category contribution
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Compare different payment methods
- Identify profitable product sub-categories
- Monitor quantity and Average Order Value (AOV)

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Profit & Loss by Month

Displays monthly profit and loss performance and helps identify:

- High-profit months
- Loss-making months
- Seasonal changes in profitability
- Overall monthly performance

### 2. Top States

Shows the states contributing the highest sales:

- Maharashtra
- Madhya Pradesh
- Uttar Pradesh
- Gujarat

### 3. Quantity by Category

The category distribution highlights the contribution of:

- Clothing
- Electronics
- Furniture

### 4. Top Customers

The dashboard identifies high-value customers based on their contribution to sales/quantity.

### 5. Quantity by Payment Mode

Payment methods analyzed include:

- COD
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- EMI

This helps understand customer payment preferences.

### 6. Profit by Sub-Category

The dashboard compares profitability across product sub-categories such as:

- Printers
- Bookcases
- Saree
- Accessories
- Tables

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to analyze the data based on:

- Quarter
- Overall selection

Users can switch between different quarters to analyze changes in sales and profitability.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Some important observations from the dashboard include:

- **Clothing** contributes the largest share of quantity among the displayed categories.
- **Maharashtra** is the leading state among the top states shown.
- **Printers** generate the highest profit among the displayed sub-categories.
- **COD** is the most commonly used payment mode.
- Monthly profitability varies significantly, with some months showing losses.
- A small group of top customers contributes significantly to overall business performance.
